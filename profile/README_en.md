# 👋 Hey there! We are NRT Corp.
> *">redefining possibilities_"*

We are not an actual corporation. We don't have "effective managers," KPIs, soul-crushing Zoom meetings, or plans to conquer the financial market. We gathered here for one simple reason: **we just love building stuff that we use ourselves and that thousands of others enjoy.**

For us, open-source isn't just a bullet point on a resume; it's a lifestyle. We believe that software should be free, code should be clean, and technology should be accessible to every enthusiast—without subscriptions or hidden tracking.

---

## 🚀 What are we up to?

We build, break, and fix things. For the most part, our projects are born from a single thought: *"Man, existing software is such a bloated, proprietary, commercial mess. We need to rewrite this properly."*

---

## 💡 Our Manifesto (or what we believe in)

1.  **People > Clout.** We build software for the person behind the screen, not for headlines in tech news.
2.  **No Bloatware.** Programs should do their job quickly, efficiently, and locally. We value privacy and your hardware's resources.
3.  **Respect for the Old School & Customization.** If a system can be tweaked, patched, or run on a potato—we'll make it happen.
4.  **The Code Belongs to Everyone.** Found a bug? Fix it. Got an idea? Pitch it. We welcome anyone ready to make the open-source world a little bit better.

---

## 🤝 Join Us!

We couldn't care less how old you are, what degree you hold, or if you work at FAANG. If you are passionate about coding, love digging through someone else's source code, and want to make a real impact—you're already one of us.

**What you can do right now:**
*   **Issues:** Found a bug in our projects or have a killer idea? Don't stay silent, open a ticket!
*   **Pull Requests:** See a way to optimize code or add a useful feature? Fork it and send a PR. We review everything and give constructive feedback (as long as we're in a good mood :D).
*   **Chat:** Drop by to hang out, talk about Linux, custom ROMs, code, or just life in general.
